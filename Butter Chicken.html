Butter Chicken

North Indian

40 mins

A rich and creamy chicken curry made with tomatoes, butter and aromatic spices. A classic Indian dish loved worldwide!

Ingredients

Chicken (boneless)

500 g

Tomatoes (puree)

3 medium

Onion

2 medium

Garlic

6 cloves

Ginger

1 inch

Butter

4 tbsp

Fresh cream

1/2 cup

Spices (see below)

Step-by-Step Instruction

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, lemon juice and spices. Keep for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat butter in a pan, add onions and cook until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook until oil separates.
  5. Add marinated chicken and cook for 10 minutes.
  6. Add cream, butter and simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.
