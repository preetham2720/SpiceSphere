Aloo Paratha

North Indian

  30 mins

Soft whole wheat flatbreads stuffed with spiced mashed potato and cooked golden with butter. A hearty Punjabi breakfast best enjoyed with curd and pickle!

Ingredients

Whole wheat flour (atta)

2 cup

Potatoes (boiled)

3 medium

Green chillies

2

Garlic

1 inch

Fresh coriander

2 tbsp

Cumin powder, garam masala

1 tsp each

Salt

to taste

Butter or ghee

4 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instruction

  1. Knead the atta with salt and water into a soft dough. Cover and rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Mash the boiled potatoes and mix with chopped chillies, ginger, coriander, spices and salt.
  3. Divide the dough into balls and roll each into a small disc.
  4. Place a portion of filling in the centre and seal the edges tightly.
  5. Dust with flour and gently roll out into a round paratha.
  6. Cook on a hot tawa, brushing both sides with butter, for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden.
  7. Top with a little more butter and serve hot with curd and pickle.

Chef's Tip

Use hung curd for a thick marinade that clings to the paneer, and leave a little space between pieces on the skewer so they char instead of steam. For a tandoor-style smoky finish, try the dhungar method with a hot piece of charcoal.
