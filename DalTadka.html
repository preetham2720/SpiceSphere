Dal Tadka

North Indian

  40 mins

Yellow lentils simmered until creamy and finished with a sizzling tempering of ghee, garlic, cumin and red chillies. Everyday comfort food that pairs perfectly with rice or roti!

Ingredients

Toor dal (split pigeon peas)

1 cup

Onion

1 medium

Tomatoes

2 medium

Garlic

6 cloves

Ghee

2 tbsp

Cumin seeds

1 tsp

Dried red chillies

2

Turmeric, chilli powder

1/2 tsp each

Step-by-Step Instruction

  1. Rinse the dal and pressure cook with 3 cups water, turmeric and salt for 4 to 5 whistles until soft.
  2. Whisk the cooked dal lightly until creamy and keep it warm.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp ghee in a pan and cook the chopped onion until golden brown.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and chilli powder and cook until soft and the oil separates.
  5. Stir this masala into the dal and simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. For the tadka, heat the remaining ghee, crackle the cumin, then add garlic and dried red chillies until golden.
  7. Pour the sizzling tadka over the dal, garnish with coriander and serve hot.

Chef's Tip

Use hung curd for a thick marinade that clings to the paneer, and leave a little space between pieces on the skewer so they char instead of steam. For a tandoor-style smoky finish, try the dhungar method with a hot piece of charcoal.
